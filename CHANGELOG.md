# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (19 October 2026)

* The player can be dragged by its background. Drop it near the menu-bar icon and it snaps back into place.
* Connecting or disconnecting a display no longer strands the player: it reattaches under the menu-bar icon, or, if you'd placed it yourself, is kept on screen. A position saved on a display that's no longer connected is ignored.
* Fixed: a black YouTube box could appear in the corner of the screen after a display change, even when no video was playing. The hidden window that keeps YouTube audio going is now invisible, and only exists while a video is loaded.
